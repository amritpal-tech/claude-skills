## Voice in one line

We talk to the C-suite like a fellow founder — take the tangle of nine motions and
hand back one clear plan.

## Who we write to

The buying committee at **B2B SaaS building outbound**:
- **Economic buyers** — CEO / Founder / CRO
- **Champions** — Head / VP / Director of Sales

Founder to founder. To the person accountable for the number, not the person
running the tool.

## The six voice principles

1. **Lead with the problem they feel.** Open on the pipeline pain the reader
   already carries — stalled sales, thin resources, channels that don't convert.
   Name it in the first line, then earn the room.
2. **Say it like the most senior person.** No junior filler, no walls of text —
   the clarity a busy executive expects.
3. **Make the complex simple.** Nine motions, one plan. Take a tangled go-to-market
   and hand back a single clear next step. If the reader feels the complexity, we
   haven't done the work.
4. **Concrete over abstract.** Trade adjectives for numbers. Not "impressions grew
   a lot" — 14.9k to 97.2k, month over month. A real figure beats a confident one.
5. **One decision per page.** A post, a page, a proposal — each asks the reader to
   make a single call. If you're presenting two decisions, you have two assets.
6. **End with the next step, not a slogan.** Close on something the reader can do —
   book the call, claim the free GTM strategy, read the plan. The last line points
   forward. It never coasts on a tagline.

## Banned words — never ship

`unlock` · `leverage` · `elevate` · `seamless` · `supercharge` · `game-changer` ·
`cutting-edge` · `holistic` · `synergy` · `delve` · `robust` · `revolutionary` ·
`in today's landscape`

Also strip:
- "AI" as a buzzword — name the product (**agentic AI SDR**, **AI SEO**) or say
  "agentic". Never "AI-powered" filler.
- Em-dash overuse.
- Rule-of-three padding.
- The "it's not X, it's Y" reflex.

**The test:** if a line could have been written about any vendor, rewrite it until
it could only be ours.

## The nine motions — naming is FIXED, use it exactly

| # | Motion | One line |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Inbound-Led** | Turn demand that already exists into pipeline — content, search, and hand-raisers who came looking. |
| 02 | **Outbound-Led** | Reach the buyers who fit before they raise a hand. Targeted, sequenced, signal-driven outreach. |
| 03 | **Sales-Led Growth (SLG)** | A rep-driven motion for considered deals — qualification, discovery, and a defined path to close. |
| 04 | **Product-Led Growth (PLG)** | Let the product do the selling — free trials, self-serve, and usage that turns into revenue. |
| 05 | **Account-Based (ABX)** | Concentrate on named accounts. Orchestrate every touch across the buying committee toward one deal. |

| 07 | **Community-Led** | Build a place buyers return to. Trust and word of mouth compound into qualified pipeline. |
| 08 | **Event-Led** | Use moments — field, digital, and roadshow — to open and advance the deals that matter most. |
| 09 | **Customer-Led Expansion** | Grow the accounts you already won. Adoption, renewal, and expansion drive most of the revenue. |

- **The discovery layer:** AI SEO feeds every motion.
- **The operator:** one agentic AI SDR runs the right ones for you.

This is the whole service, not a menu to pick from.

## The conversion flow — from search to signed

Value-first. A buyer arrives with a pipeline problem and leaves the first call with
a plan already in hand.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 01 | **Search / AI SEO** — a senior buyer looks for an answer to a pipeline problem; our AI SEO layer puts us in front of them. |
| 02 | **Homepage mirrors the problem** — the page reflects their exact pipeline pain back to them. |
| 03 | **CTA: free audit, analysis + GTM strategy** — real work, not a demo. |
| 04 | **Lead form** — four fields: name, email, website, your pipeline challenge. |
| 05 | **Agentic skill builds the plan** — drafts a tailored GTM Strategy & Proposal doc, specific to their site and challenge. |
| 06 | **Automated email + book a time** — the doc is delivered with a clear "book a time" CTA. |
| 07 | **Qualified first call** — specific, not exploratory. They arrive with the plan and a reason to act. |
| 08 | **90-day nurture** — playbooks, resource links, and prospect-specific plays the AI SDR runs over the next quarter. |

**Every asset lands on one capture path: the free GTM Audit / free GTM Strategy.**
