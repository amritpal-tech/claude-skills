## Contrast pairs that ship

| Pair | Ratio | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Ink `#0C0F1A` on White | 18.1:1 | AAA — default reading pair |
| White on Ink `#0C0F1A` | 18.1:1 | AAA — dark surface pair |
| White on Blue `#2F5BFF` | 4.9:1 | AA — primary CTA |
| White on Red `#FF3B1F` | 3.4:1 | **AA LARGE only** — large text & fills, never body |
| Ink on Mist `#EEF1F8` | 16.4:1 | AAA — panels & quiet callouts |

Every text pairing clears WCAG AA before it ships.

## Proportion — the 70 / 20 / 10 balance

- **70% neutral** (white · mist · ink) — holds the page and does the quiet work.
- **20% Electric Blue** — moves the eye toward the idea and the action.
- **10% signal** (red · green) — marks the one point that matters: the myth or the win.

## Semantic rule

| Colour | Means |
|---|---|
| Blue | key idea, the answer |
| Red | myth, old rule, risk |
| Green | the win, proof |
| Ink | depth, gravity |
| White | clarity, room |

## Hard rules

- **One hero background per surface.** A page, section, or slide gets a single
  dominant colour. Never stack two heroes side by side.
- **Body text is never blue.** Blue carries the idea and the action. Reading copy
  stays ink on light or white on dark.
- **Signal colours earn their place.** Red, green and mint are reserved for the
  myth, the win, and the metric — not decoration.
- **Flat fills only.** Gradients blur contrast ratios, wash out the signal colours,
  and read as decoration rather than meaning. One solid colour per surface.
