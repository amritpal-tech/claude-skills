# DevCommX standing keyword exclusion list

Source: **DevCommX Keyword Strategy v1.0, 3 August 2026** + the keyword research sheet
(Ahrefs CSV, Ubersuggest competitor + seed exports, GSC, SERP research).

Every term here was cut for a **named reason**, not because the volume was too small.
Recorded so the decision is not re-litigated every quarter.

> **Count note.** The strategy doc headlines "43 keywords" across four categories
> (job seekers, tool queries, wrong buyer, informational). Those four total **39**
> individually-named terms in the research sheet. The full sheet names **48** terms
> across **8** categories, plus 4 bulk blocks. The list below is the complete set as
> it appears in the sheet — worth reconciling with the doc's 43 at the next review.

**42,880 searches a month between them — roughly 36× the entire Tier A list.**
DevCommX is choosing 1,170 searches over 42,880 because the smaller number is buyers.

## Conditional revisits

Only two terms have a stated path back. Both need the **reason** to change, not the volume:

- `b2b marketing company` — revisit once domain rating passes **40**.
- Tier C agency-alternatives terms (`belkins alternatives`, `martal group alternatives`,
  `belkins vs martal group`, `cience alternatives`, `salesroads alternatives`,
  `callbox alternatives`, `best outbound agency for saas`) — **UNVERIFIED volume**, gated
  on the Wave 3 validation task. Any term with no measurable volume and no existing
  impressions gets cut and its page is not built.
