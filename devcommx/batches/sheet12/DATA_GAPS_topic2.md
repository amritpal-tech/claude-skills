# Data gaps: topic 2, ai-marketing-budget-b2b-allocation

Recorded 2026-09-10. Every number in the draft comes from a named, inline linked third
party source. The items below are places where the brief asked for a DevCommX first party
figure that does not exist, and where a published third party benchmark or a reader run
method is standing in instead. None of them blocks publishing: each spot reads correctly
as written, and each one names whose number it is.

## 1. Before and after AI spend breakdown (brief: "a before/after spend breakdown")
DevCommX has no measured client dataset of AI marketing budget allocation by category.
The draft therefore presents the six line comparison table as an explicitly labelled
model, not measured data. The prose says so in plain text: "the split below is a model,
not a measured DevCommX dataset, and you should treat it as a structure to fill with your
own line items." The only hard percentages attached to it are Gartner's, cited inline:
AI at 15.3 percent of marketing budgets, 21.3 percent among AI ready organisations,
marketing budgets at 7.8 percent of revenue versus 8.9 percent for the AI ready cohort.

**What would replace it:** DevCommX aggregate allocation across client AI line items,
tagged to the six categories in the table, with sample size and the period measured.

## 2. Named example of the 80 percent paying off (brief: "one named example")
No DevCommX client result can be named beyond the single sanctioned proof point. The
draft uses two named third party cohorts instead, both cited inline:
- Gartner's AI ready cohort, which allocates more to AI and runs larger marketing budgets
  as a share of revenue.
- McKinsey's AI high performer group, roughly 6 percent of respondents, distinguished by
  workflow redesign rather than tool selection, against about 21 percent of generative AI
  users having redesigned any workflow and about 39 percent reporting enterprise level
  EBIT impact.

**What would replace it:** one DevCommX client, with permission, where funding research,
review or distribution moved a stated pipeline metric, with before and after values and
the measurement window.

## 3. Self audit benchmark ratios
The 20 minute self audit tells the reader to compute their own generation share of total
AI spend and flags "above half" as the imbalance threshold. That threshold is a practitioner
heuristic stated as such, not a measured distribution.

**What would replace it:** the observed distribution of generation share across the AI
budgets DevCommX has audited, so the draft can state a real median rather than a rule of
thumb.

## Sanctioned claim usage
"40+ qualified demos in approximately 6 weeks" appears exactly once, in the CTA paragraph.
No other DevCommX performance number appears anywhere in the draft.

## Research note
Gartner, McKinsey and Businesswire are blocked by the network egress proxy in this
environment, so their figures come from search result summaries of the named pages rather
than from a direct fetch of them. The URLs cited are the canonical pages for those
findings and should be spot checked by a human before publish.
