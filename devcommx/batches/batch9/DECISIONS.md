verbatim copies of live competitor headlines from four different sites.

CMS slug inventory, not against the sheet alone — and that check changed one verdict.

## Held (7)

**Topics 1, 2, 3 — dropped, off-ICP and copied.** UX/UI and "pretty website" digital
strategy are grm.digital's, enterprise SEO platforms is BrightEdge's. All three serve
web-design or enterprise-SEO buyers. Topic 2 is worse than off-topic: it is a vendor
arguing for the category that vendor sells.

**Topics 4 and 7 — dropped, duplicate each other and three live pages.** Both land on
`llmo-vs-seo-vs-geo-vs-aeo`, `aeo-vs-geo-difference-strategy-guide` and
`aeo-services-vs-seo-services-contracts`. Topic 4 also opens with "Unlocking", which the
brand book bans. The slot is better spent refreshing the existing acronym page.

**Topic 8 — not a page.** Third appearance of the assistant-versus-agent question;
`ai-sdr-sales-autopilot-vs-copilot` and `what-is-agentic-marketing-b2b-guide` are both live.

**Topic 6 — dropped, and this overrides the sheet.** The sheet said RETITLE and scored
Originality 1/5 against two collisions. The inventory shows a third it never mentions:
`how-to-get-saas-recommended-by-chatgpt` is live, and its H2s map almost one-to-one onto
topic 6's proposed structure — four levers that decide whether you are named, a 30-day
build sequence, and measuring inclusion rate rather than traffic. Retitling topic 6 would
produce a near-duplicate of a page that already exists. There is no scope left for it.

## What the sheet got wrong

It states, for topic 10, that "Claude and others" is "the genuinely new section. Nothing
live covers it." Both `how-to-get-cited-by-claude-ai` and `how-to-get-cited-by-google-gemini`
are live. This cuts both ways: it removes a section topic 10 was counting on, and it
strengthens the pillar case, because five per-platform children now exist with no hub
above them. Topic 10 is drafted as that hub.

## Conventions applied

**Heading capitalisation: Title Case for every H2, H3 and H4.** Measured across the
79-draft corpus: of the 53 blogs that are internally consistent, 42 use Title Case, and the
final CTA H2 is Title Case in 63 of 79. The 26 internally mixed blogs are the
inconsistency this batch's instruction is aimed at. The sheet's recommended headings are
sentence case; wording and order are kept, capitalisation is normalised to Title Case.

**Every internal link target verified live** against the CMS inventory before drafting.
`pillar-pages-for-ai-search` is deliberately *not* linked from topic 10, because it will be
an unpublished draft and linking it would create a 404. Add that link once it is live.

**Conclusions are structurally different** across all three, and checked against the
Batch 7 conclusions so the batches do not converge either.
