## Source caveats

7. **McKinsey State of AI, AI incident rate.** Secondary summaries of the November 2025
   report cite "51 percent of firms report AI incidents". The primary PDF was not fetched
   within the research budget, so the post uses the softer, well-attested framing ("most
   organisations already experience negative consequences from AI use") and cites the
   65 percent versus 23 percent human-in-the-loop figure, which appears consistently
   across summaries.

8. **Bridge Group 2025 figures** (351 implementations, 45 dials a day, about five quality
   conversations, 1.9 year median tenure, roughly 63 percent quota attainment) come from
   the report's public summary pages. The full report is gated and was not downloaded, so
   these were not verified against the primary document.

9. **Gartner rep-free buying share.** Two figures circulate: 67 percent (Gartner, March
   2026 survey, 646 buyers, fielded August to September 2025) and an older 75 percent
   figure repeated by aggregators. The post uses 67 percent and attributes it to the
   March 2026 survey.
