### Research brief
- **What happened:** HubSpot's Nov 4 results frame the company's direction as the "Outcomes Era". Its agents already bill per outcome in HubSpot Credits.
- **Pricing as reported by third-party breakdowns:** Customer Agent: 50 credits (about $0.50) per resolved conversation on text channels; Prospecting Agent: 100 credits (about $1) per lead it recommends outreach for; capacity packs of 1,000 credits for $10, or pay-as-you-go at $0.01 per credit above the limit; included credits reset monthly and don't roll over; standard enrichment reportedly no longer consumes credits. One source dates the outcome-based change to 14 April 2026; another lists an older $1.00 Customer Agent price.
- **Sources:** [HubSpot IR, Q3 2026 results](https://ir.hubspot.com/news-releases/news-release-details/hubspot-reports-q3-2026-results) · [eesel on HubSpot AI agent pricing](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/hubspot-ai-agent-pricing-2026) · [Simple Machines on tracking and capping credits](https://www.simplemachinesmarketing.com/blog/hubspot-credits-in-2026-how-to-track-cap-and-budget-them) (third-party; HubSpot's catalog not opened)
- **Why it matters to his reader:** outcome billing makes the CRM invoice a function of agent behaviour. RevOps inherits the definitions, the logging and the reconciliation. It continues his "if an agent can act, it needs an identity" thread.

### Post
<!-- post:pankaj format:single-image ask:repost-if type:news -->
Outcome pricing turns your CRM bill into a meter.

HubSpot's agents already bill that way. Third-party pricing breakdowns put it at:

↳ Customer Agent: 50 credits, about $0.50, per resolved conversation
↳ Prospecting Agent: 100 credits, about $1, per lead it recommends outreach for
↳ Overage: $0.01 per credit, and included credits reset monthly

On November 4, HubSpot's Q3 release said it is transforming the company "to lead in the Outcomes Era." Expect more of the bill to work like this.

A meter you don't instrument is a bill you can't explain. Here's how I'd wire it:

1. Write down what counts. "Resolved" and "recommended" are the vendor's definitions until you check them against your own.
2. Log every billed outcome to a record, with the agent as the actor. If it can bill, it needs an identity.
3. Reconcile weekly. Billed outcomes on one side, your own count on the other.
4. Alert on burn rate. Included credits don't roll over, so a slow month wastes them and a busy one hits overage.
5. Name an owner for the credit budget. Usually that's RevOps, whether anyone said so or not.

Honest limit: these prices come from third-party breakdowns, and they differ slightly. Check HubSpot's catalog for your contract.

Outcome pricing is fair when both sides count the same way.

Repost if your team runs agents and nobody owns the credit budget.
