## Post
<!-- post:pankaj format:carousel ask:save-this type:evergreen -->
Outbound is a six-level game. Every level has a boss.

The boss is whatever breaks. Beat it with plumbing and you reach the next level.

Here's the map I'd build.

Level 1: Signals.
The job: spot accounts that just did something. A key hire, a funding round, a new tool.
The boss: stale signals. Stamp every signal with a date and drop it after 30 days.

Level 2: The list.
The job: turn signal accounts into verified contacts.
The boss: bounces. Verify every email before it enters a sequence, and write the result to a field.

Level 3: The message.
The job: one reason to talk, tied to the signal.
The boss: mail merge dressed up as personalization. If the first line fits any account, rewrite it.

Level 4: Replies.
The job: sort every reply into interested, not now, wrong person or opt-out.
The boss: replies that sit. Route "interested" to a named owner within minutes, thread attached.

Level 5: The scoreboard.
The job: one daily report, pulled from the CRM.
The boss: numbers that don't match. One definition of "meeting booked", one owner.

Level 6: Inbound.
The job: content that brings back accounts outbound already warmed.
The boss: no join. Tag inbound leads with the outbound account, so you can see both halves.

Skip a level and its boss follows you to the next one.

One tool, one job, one field at every handoff.

Save this before you rebuild your outbound.
<!-- /post -->
