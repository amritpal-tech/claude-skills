Make supplier dependence a decision.

Four questions for the leadership team this month.
01
Which revenue-critical workflows depend on a single model provider, directly or through a vendor?
02
Can each vendor tell you which models it runs on, and how long a switch would take?
03
Do your contracts give notice on pricing changes and a clean way to take your data out?
04
Who reads a key supplier's public filings as part of the vendor review?
Depending on one supplier can be a sound decision.
It becomes a risk when nobody made it.
