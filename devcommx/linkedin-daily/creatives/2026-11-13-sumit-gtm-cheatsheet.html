A sharp ICP in 5 steps

  • 1
    Start with your best 20 closed-won deals.
  • 2
    Write their pain in their own words, from call notes.
  • 3
    Add the fit: size, stack, region, buying team.
  • 4
    Add one live signal: hiring, funding, a new tool.
  • 5
    Re-check it against new wins every quarter.
THE SHARPNESS TESTCan a new SDR build a list of 100 accounts from it in an hour? If not, it isn't sharp yet.
