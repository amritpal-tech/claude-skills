### Post
<!-- post:vignesh format:text ask:sharp-question type:news -->
Anthropic is public now. Your AI vendor review should change.

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share on November 18, above its marketed range. CNBC called it the largest offering on record. Trading began the next day.

Set the stock aside. The question for operators is what it means for companies that run revenue on these models.

After 23 years of building B2B systems, I read it as a supplier change.

Much of the modern GTM stack runs on a handful of model providers. Often the buyer doesn't know which one sits under each tool.

A private supplier answers to its investors and its roadmap. A public one also answers to the quarter. That can bring steadier disclosure. It can also bring pricing and terms that move with results. That part is my read.

Four questions I'd put to a leadership team this month:

1. Which revenue-critical workflows depend on a single model provider, directly or through a vendor?
2. Can each vendor tell you which models it runs on, and how long a switch would take?
3. Do your contracts give notice on pricing changes and a clean way to take your data out?
4. Who reads a key supplier's public filings as part of the vendor review?

Depending on one supplier can be a sound decision. It becomes a risk when nobody made it.

Which of your revenue workflows would stall if one model provider changed its terms next quarter?
