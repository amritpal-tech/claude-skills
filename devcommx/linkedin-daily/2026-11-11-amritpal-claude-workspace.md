## Research brief
- **Claude Sheets, Whiteboards and Data ([claude.com, Nov 10](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data), opened):**
  - Whiteboards (beta): a shared board where the team brainstorms with Claude. Turns spoken plans into boards with timelines, owners and dates; groups teammates' notes by theme and drafts action items; exports to a doc or deck, or moves to Lucid, Miro or Vani by Zoho.
  - Sheets (beta): "a spreadsheet Claude builds and edits with you". Tabs, formulas, formatting and drop-downs from a prompt; Anthropic's examples are "a campaign tracker or a hiring budget". Edits are outlined. Basic features for now; export to .xlsx or CSV, or use Claude for Excel.
  - Data (generally available, formerly Claude Dashboards): data stories, certified queries and live sharing (the last two in beta). Certified metric definitions carry a "certified" badge. Needs a data connector (a warehouse or CRM).
  - Availability: all paid plans, on web, desktop and mobile. Team plans: on by default. Enterprise: Sheets and Whiteboards off by default; Data on by default from Nov 19.
- **Interactive connectors ([claude.com, Nov 10](https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool), opened):** apps open beside the chat or full screen, and Claude works in them while you approve changes. Available now includes Asana, Canva, Carta, Dropbox, monday.com, Smartsheet, Vercel, Webflow, Adobe, Atlassian (Confluence), Box, HubSpot and Notion. HubSpot example: prioritise pipeline tasks and follow-ups, then act on them. Adobe: photo retouching and PDF editing. Figma and Shopify are "also building integrations". Rolling out on web and desktop, on all plans.
- **Not used:** Claude Meetings. Coverage says beta on Team and Enterprise; Anthropic's own article still says coming soon. Left out until Anthropic's page confirms.
- **Why it matters to the reader:** a B2B marketing lead's campaign lives in six tools. This is the first time Claude can open most of them in one thread, and Claude Data gives one place to define a metric like MQL once.
- **Angle:** the tab switch is where campaign context gets lost. Test one workflow per tool, and keep the system of record where it is.

The life of a campaign, in six tabs:

1. Brainstorm it on a whiteboard.
2. Plan it in a spreadsheet.
3. Design it in Canva.
4. Publish it in Webflow.
5. Work the leads in HubSpot.
6. Report it on a dashboard.

On November 10, Anthropic brought all six into Claude.

↳ Claude Whiteboards (beta) turns a spoken plan into a board with owners and dates, then groups the team's notes and drafts action items.

↳ Claude Sheets (beta) builds a spreadsheet from a prompt. Anthropic's own example is a campaign tracker.

↳ Claude Data, now generally available, adds certified metric definitions and data stories once you connect a warehouse or CRM.

↳ Interactive connectors open Canva, Webflow, HubSpot, Adobe and Notion beside the chat, and Claude works in them while you approve the changes.

Here's the actual mechanism. Every tab switch used to mean re-explaining the campaign. When the tools open in the same thread, the context comes with them.

Here's how I'd test it this week:

1. Build next month's campaign tracker in Sheets from your brief, then check every formula.
2. Run one planning call into a Whiteboard and compare its action items with your own notes.
3. Define MQL once as a certified metric in Claude Data, so every dashboard reports the same number.
4. Keep your system of record where it is. Claude can work inside those tools from the thread.

One honest disqualifier: Sheets and Whiteboards are betas with basic features, and the connectors are still rolling out.

Every tab switch is a small tax on the campaign.

Which tab would you close first?
