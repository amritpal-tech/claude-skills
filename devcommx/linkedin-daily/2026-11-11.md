### Post
<!-- post:pankaj format:text ask:sharp-question type:news -->
Your AI agent closes the chat. Your CRM never hears about it.

That's the pattern in Invoca's 2026 Broken Funnel Report, out November 10. It surveyed 451 US B2C marketing leaders about customer-facing AI agents on phone, chat and text.

↳ 74% can't track whether an agent-handled lead turns into a sale.

↳ Only 26% can tie agent conversations to the campaign or ad that drove them.

↳ Only 19% reliably send conversation outcomes back to their ad platforms.

That's consumer brands. B2B teams putting agents on the website or in inbound face the same plumbing question.

The gap sits in the join between the chat, the CRM and the campaign.

Here's how I'd wire it:

1. Capture the source at the first message. UTMs, click ID and landing page go on the conversation record before the agent says a word.
2. Give every conversation an ID and write it to the contact in your CRM.
3. Write the outcome as fields: qualified, meeting booked, disqualified reason. Free text doesn't report.
4. Link the conversation to the opportunity, so revenue can roll back to the chat and the campaign.
5. Send outcomes back to the ad platforms as offline conversions. Otherwise they optimise for whatever you do send.

Here is the line that splits this room: if an AI agent can't show up in your pipeline report, it's a cost centre with a nice transcript.

How many of your agent conversations can you trace to a closed deal today?

<!-- post:amritpal format:text ask:save-this type:news -->
The costliest core update mistake happens in week one.

Google started rolling out its November 2026 core update on November 10. It's global, covers all languages, and may take up to two weeks.

It's the third core update this year, after March and May.

The mistake: reading the first days of movement as the verdict, and rewriting pages while rankings are still settling.

Google's own guidance points the other way. Wait until the rollout is complete. Then wait at least a full week more before comparing Search Console data against a baseline from before it started.

Here's how I'd run the next three weeks on a B2B site:

1. Annotate November 10 in your analytics and in your Search Console exports today.
2. Save a baseline now: clicks, impressions and average position for your top 50 pages and your money pages (pricing, comparisons, demo).
3. Change nothing structural until Google marks the rollout complete.
4. When it's done, wait a week, then compare like for like: same days of the week, same page groups.
5. If a page group dropped, read the pages that replaced you before you rewrite yours.

One honest disqualifier: Google published no new guidance for this update, so nobody knows yet what it rewards. Anyone telling you on day two is guessing.

During a core update, patience is part of the strategy.

Save this for the day the dashboard says complete.
