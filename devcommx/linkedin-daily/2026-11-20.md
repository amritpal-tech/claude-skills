Clay's new agent shows you the bill before it runs.

Clay put its global agent into open beta on November 19, on paid Launch, Growth and Enterprise plans.

You chat with it in the Agent tab. It plans a play and builds it across Search, Audiences, Workflows and Sequencer.

Two details matter more than the demo.

5. Keep a person on the first send of any sequence it builds.

The part most teams will skip: an agent that can edit your ICP can change who you target without anyone touching a filter.

Review Knowledge Hub edits the way you'd review a field change in the CRM.

Approve the sample. Then the spend.

Save this before you hand Clay's agent its first play.
