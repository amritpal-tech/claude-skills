# Profile Rewrite Templates & Scaffolds

Fill-in-the-blank scaffolds for each profile field. Always produce 2–3 options per field and recommend one. Replace every bracket; never ship a bracket. Mark missing proof as `[NEEDS: …]`.

The positioning equation that feeds every field: **`Outcome + ICP + Mechanism`**.

---

## Headline (220 chars max)

The most important field — read by humans *and* 360Brew, and the template for every post hook.

**Scaffold A — outcome-first:**
`I help [ICP] [achieve outcome] — with [mechanism].`
> *I help dev-tool founders turn a great product into real pipeline — with content + SEO/GEO systems they can run themselves.*

**Scaffold B — mechanism-as-identity:**
`[Mechanism] for [ICP]. [Outcome, with a number if possible].`
> *Distribution systems for dev-tool founders. From 0 to 40k monthly readers without a marketing team.*

**Scaffold C — contrarian positioning:**
`[ICP] don't have a [X] problem — they have a [real problem]. I fix the second one.`
> *Dev-tool founders don't have a product problem — they have a distribution problem. I fix the second one.*

**Avoid:** title soup ("Founder | Builder | Coffee | Dad"), buzzword stacks, anything not keyword-consistent with what they post.

---

## Banner (one line + soft CTA)

The above-the-fold pitch. Design carries it, but the *line* matters.

**Scaffold:** `[Who you help] → [the result]. [Soft CTA].`
> *Helping dev-tool founders build distribution that compounds. → Follow for the system. / Free framework in Featured ↓*

---

## About / Summary (2,600 chars max; first ~3 lines are above "…see more")

Structure, in strict order:

```
[LINE 1–3 — THE HOOK / READER'S PAIN, in their words. Wins the "…see more" click.]
You built something great. Nobody's showing up. And "post more" isn't a strategy.

[THE REFRAME — name the real problem.]
The gap isn't your product. It's that you have no distribution system — and founder energy doesn't scale.

[THE MECHANISM — your repeatable system, not a vague claim.]
Here's what I do: [step 1] → [step 2] → [step 3]. A system you can run in [time], not a retainer you can't measure.

[PROOF — real numbers, named results, artefacts. Use [NEEDS: …] if missing.]
[NEEDS: real metric — e.g. "Took X from 2k to 38k monthly readers in 90 days."]

[POV / HUMANIZER — the spiky thing you actually believe + a small true detail.]
I think most dev-tool content is written for the wrong person. I write for the founder scrolling between Slack and their IDE.

[CTA — one action only.]
Follow for the distribution system. Or grab the [framework] in Featured.
```

**Voice rules:** first-person, plain spoken language (explain it to a founder over coffee), no "I'm excited to share," no motivational filler, no AI-flavoured corporate tone. One idea per line; generous white space.

---

## Featured (3–4 items — proof + lead capture)

Curate so a visit converts. Prioritise save-worthy assets (saves = ~5× a like).

| Slot | What goes here |
|---|---|
| 1 | Highest-performing / most save-worthy post (the framework or checklist they reference) |
| 2 | Lead magnet (the [framework], checklist, or teardown) |
| 3 | Case study or proof piece (real numbers) |
| 4 | Booking link / clear next step |

---

## Experience & Skills (expertise signal)

- **Experience entries:** rewrite the current role's blurb in the same ICP + mechanism + outcome language as the headline. Lead with what the ICP gets, not internal duties.
- **Skills:** list the exact keywords the person posts about (e.g. *developer marketing, content distribution, SEO, GEO, demand generation*). The goal is a tight profile-to-content keyword match so 360Brew's expertise-match signal fires clean.

---

## The coherence checklist (run before shipping)

- [ ] Headline, About line 1, and the person's typical post hook all carry the **same** positioning.
- [ ] Every keyword in Skills/Experience appears in the topics they actually post about.
- [ ] No claim in About lacks proof (or a `[NEEDS: …]` flag).
- [ ] One identity, not five. A reader can say in one sentence what this person is "the [X] person" for.
- [ ] Voice is first-person and human — no corporate/AI sheen.
