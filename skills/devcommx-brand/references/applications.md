## Single-image post — 1080 × 1080

One post, one line.

- **Brand ink surface.** Dark ink background, white logo, sky-tint kicker. Hatch
  motif fades in **from the top-right corner only**.
- **One line, front and centre.** The message is the hero. Logo top-left, source
  bottom-left, everything else gets out of the way.
- **Senior, not shouty.** Written for a founder, CRO, or VP Sales at an
  outbound-led B2B SaaS. Calm, specific, plain — never hype or a rounded-up claim.
- **Legible at thumbnail size.** The line must read in the feed at roughly a third
  of this size. If it blurs small, cut words until it holds.

Footer line, mono, muted: `devcommx.com · Go-to-market, made simple`.

## Deck template

Three slide types, one grid. Every slide keeps the same margins, an eyebrow, and a
footer with the mark.

| Type | Surface | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | Ink, white mark | Two-line sentence-case headline, mono sub-line (`Outbound GTM for B2B SaaS · 2026`) |
| **Content** | Light | Eyebrow, one-line argument as the H2, 3 bullets with blue square markers — each bullet is `**Bold lead** — explanation` |
| **Data** | Light | Eyebrow, headline, one hero stat in an ink tile + one flat chart |

**Deck rules — the four constants:**
1. One hero colour per slide. Blue marks the answer, ink carries depth.
2. Eyebrow top-left, mark bottom-left, page number bottom-right — every slide.
3. Numbers are always real and rounded. **No stat without a source.**
4. One system, three jobs: set the room with the title, name the pipeline problem on
   content, land it with a real number on data — then repeat.

## Website

The homepage mirrors a B2B SaaS revenue leader's **pipeline problem, not our service
list.** One promise, one blue action, one route into the free GTM Audit.

- **Light is the default surface** — clarity first, navy+red logo, one blue CTA.
- **Dark is for the fold** on outbound-campaign and account pages built for one buyer.
- **Never two competing CTAs.** Blue starts the free GTM Audit; the text link
  explains the motions.
- Hero pattern: eyebrow (`OUTBOUND GTM FOR B2B SAAS`) → display headline where the
  second clause turns electric blue → body naming the pain → primary blue button +
  ghost text link → a mono proof strip (`Blog impressions 14.9k → 97.2k month over
  month · Built founder-to-founder`).
- Motion chips row: the nine motion names as outline pills, with `AI SEO` as the one
  filled blue pill.
- Hatch accent enters from one corner only.

**Conversion flow page (`/free-gtm-audit`):** three columns — *tell us the problem*
(4-field form) → *building your strategy* (checklist, progress bar) → *your GTM
strategy* (doc preview + one blue "Book a time" button). Value first, then the call.

## Ad creatives

Three formats, one system. Every creative lands on the free GTM Audit.

| Format | Size | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Square · social feed | 1080 × 1080 | Ink surface, hatch top-right, headline where the risk clause turns **Signal Red**, one-line sub, blue button |
| Landscape · display | 1200 × 450 | White, blue left rule, headline with the key clause in blue, blue button right |
| Story · vertical | 1080 × 1920 | Blue top half (headline + sub), red bottom half, white pill CTA, mono `FREE · NO DISCOVERY CALL` |

**Every ad, same discipline:**
- **One hero colour.** Red carries the risk. Blue carries the action. Never both loud.
- **One buyer in mind.** A B2B SaaS revenue leader scaling outbound — not a generic
  B2B audience.
- **Real numbers only.** 14.9k → 97.2k. No rounded-up claims, no stock hype.
- **One capture path.** Every format lands on the free GTM Audit.

## Video *(extension of the system — not a separate section in Book v3.1)*

Derived from the same rules; use it until the book adds a motion section.

- **Surface:** ink by default. Blue or red only as a full-frame hero for a single
  beat (the myth, or the CTA) — never both in the same frame.
- **Type:** Inter 800 sentence-case titles; JetBrains Mono for every number,
  timestamp, and lower-third label. Never Title Case a title card.
- **Motion:** cuts and simple position/opacity moves. No 3D, no gradients, no glows,
  no bounce easing. The diagonal hatch may wipe in from one corner as a transition.
- **Frame furniture:** eyebrow top-left, D symbol bottom-left, everything anchored
  low with the top band left as air — the same grid as a slide.
- **Numbers on screen:** mono, tabular, rounded, and labelled illustrative if they
  are illustrative.
- **Captions:** white on ink, sentence case, one idea per card.
- **Ending:** one action — the free GTM Audit — on a single end card. No slogan
  outro, no social-icon wall.
